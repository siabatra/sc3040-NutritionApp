### 1 · Generate: what is requested, and why
Seed a **curated reference table of common local hawker dishes**, with reviewed nutrition values, for the app to use when recognising and logging meals.

USDA FoodData Central covers local hawker dishes poorly. Today the app estimates them by splitting each dish into generic ingredients, which is less accurate for common local meals.
