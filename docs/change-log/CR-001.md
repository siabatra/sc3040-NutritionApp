### 1 · Generate: what is requested, and why
Replace the **Nutritionix** nutrition database with the **USDA FoodData Central (FDC) API**.

Nutritionix required a SGD 350 monthly licence and was the project's only paid nutrition dependency. USDA FDC is free and provides the calorie, protein, carbohydrate and fat values the app needs.

### 3 · Evaluate: impact
- **Cost:** removes the **SGD 350 monthly licence**. USDA FDC costs SGD 0.
- **External interface:** the nutrition lookup moves to `api.nal.usda.gov/fdc/v1/foods/search`.
- **Scope:** no feature change for users.
- **Documents updated:** SRS Sections 5 and 6, the proposal budget, and all sequence diagrams.

### 4 · Authorize: CCB decision
**Approved.**

### 5 · Implement
- `project/utils/nutritionApi.ts` now calls USDA FDC.
- The wrapper still returns the previous field names (`nf_calories`, `nf_protein`, `nf_total_carbohydrate`, `nf_total_fat`), so **no calling screen had to change**. The change stayed inside one module.
